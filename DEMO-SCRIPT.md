# Demo script — Token DD Agent (90 seconds)

For Sneaks to record. Narration is ~220 words ≈ 90 seconds at a natural pace.
Read the **bold** lines; the bracketed cues are screen direction, not spoken.
Record the live report runs *before* filming the narration, and cut them in —
if the venue network is slow, the gallery page (`/examples`) shows the same
two reports as captured snapshots and works offline.

---

**[0:00–0:12 · HOOK — landing page on screen]**

> "Every day, thousands of new Solana tokens launch — and most due
> diligence is a vibe. This agent replaces the vibe with a number,
> and shows its work."

**[0:12–0:32 · THE FLOW — paste the FLUKE address into the box, submit, report loads]**

> "Paste any contract address. The agent pulls the token's live data
> from GMGN — contract security, holder structure, price history —
> and scores it against a fixed rubric. No wallet, no keys, read-only.
> Here's a real report, run live: FLUKE — seventy-nine out of a
> hundred. Buyable, with conditions."

**[0:32–0:58 · ANATOMY — scroll the report: sub-scores, then the findings list; pause on the entrapment line]**

> "Three sections do the scoring. Contract safety: can you actually
> sell it back. Holder structure: who holds it, and how concentrated
> it is. Price action: where the price sits in its own history.
> And every deduction names the exact field it came from. This one —
> a third of its traders are tagged entrapment, minus sixteen.
> There's the field, straight from the data."

**[0:58–1:14 · THE CAP — cut to the $OV gallery card / report header]**

> "One rule outranks the math: GMGN's own rug label caps the score.
> This token's sections add up to about sixty-six — but its rug label
> is zero point nine four, so the cap cuts it to fifty-nine.
> Not advised."

**[1:14–1:30 · CLOSE — back to the landing page / gallery]**

> "Same rubric, every token, every deduction traceable to a field.
> It's the check an agent — or a human — runs before money moves.
> Token DD Agent. Not investment advice — just the receipts."

  (labeled "captured 2026-10-30") and keep the script as written.

  don't cut the wait out entirely, it reads as honest.

  the product, not fine print.
