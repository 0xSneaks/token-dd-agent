Worked examples

Three reports rendered by this app. The first two are real: scored live against GMGN through the app's own adapter (/report?address=…&live=1) and captured on 2026-10-30. Token data moves, so re-running the live links today may give different numbers — these cards are the 2026-10-30 snapshots, kept as the demo's worked examples. The third is the fictional SAMPLE used to demo the template offline.

How to read a score: three sections — contract safety, holder structure, price action — are scored 0–100 and weighted 0.45 / 0.35 / 0.20. The composite can then be capped: GMGN's own rug label caps it at 79 when the label is 0.30–0.50, and at 59 when it is 0.50 or higher (rubric Step 5B). Bands: 80+ “buyable”, 60–79 “buyable with conditions”, 40–59 “not advised”, under 40 “do not buy”. “Buyable” is a transactability / rug-exposure read, never a price forecast.
